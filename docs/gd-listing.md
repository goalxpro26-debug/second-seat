## Long description (197 words, reference only)
Second Seat is a town adventure about sharing one scooter. You and your partner cross a sun-faded repair-shop town together, but you never fully control the scooter: you steer while your partner works the throttle, and one button swaps your seats. The partner you leave behind keeps your last order and tires out, and a tired partner stalls the scooter until you swap back and let them rest. That one swap is the whole game. Twelve districts take you from Repair Row to Hilltop Garage, each with its own deliveries, traffic and missions: deliver parcels, chase a score target, or finish a district without a single stall. Earn up to three stars per district by score, save your progress, and keep riding in endless courier mode, where deliveries keep coming, traffic thickens, and three crashes end the run. Dodge cars, trucks and potholes, grab spare parts for bonus points, and slow down at delivery stops to drop off parcels. All art is hand drawn in code and all sound is synthesized, with original music, engine sounds and effects. Simple controls, keyboard or touch, and a gentle first district that teaches the swap before the town gets busy.
